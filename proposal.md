## 4. Abstract (100–150 words)

VitalSense-Next is an end-to-end health surveillance system designed for institutional environments such as hostels and care homes. The project integrates an Arduino Uno R4 WiFi wearable node equipped with MAX30102, DS18B20, and MPU-6050 sensors to monitor heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature, and fall-risk activity. Sensor data is filtered and processed on-device before being transmitted over Wi-Fi to a FastAPI backend, where it is validated, stored in TimescaleDB, and cached in Redis. A React.js dashboard provides real-time alerts, historical trends, and staff triage views for administrators, wardens, and medical personnel. The system aims to improve early detection of abnormal vitals and emergency incidents while maintaining a scalable, low-cost, and privacy-aware architecture suitable for academic and institutional deployment.
